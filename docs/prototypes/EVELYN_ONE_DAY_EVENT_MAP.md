# Evelyn One-Day Event Map

> **Superseded (2026-09-20).** The current design is [`PORTFOLIO_JUDGMENT_GAME_SPEC.md`](./PORTFOLIO_JUDGMENT_GAME_SPEC.md): a compact 8 to 12 minute portfolio
> judgment game. This event map belongs to the earlier one-day management prototype. It is kept as history and as source
> material for later slices, and its content below is unchanged.

**Status:** Superseded. (Previously: Draft paper-prototype scenario)  
**Target:** 15-20 minutes of play  
**Goal:** Produce one complete turn with attention, delegation, provenance, memory, and a player-caused consequence.

## Hidden authored truth for facilitator

The player should not receive this section during blind testing.

- Robert is dead.
- Evelyn's early Robert references are ambiguous and not enough to establish a clinical conclusion.
- The grocery delivery occurs, but butter pecan is substituted with vanilla.
- Jenny can verify physical facts but cannot diagnose Evelyn.
- Michael's boundary is genuine and should not be bypassed.
- No single missed clue should block completion.

## Starting visible state

### People
- Evelyn - available
- Jenny - scheduled for 11:30 AM, 45-minute visit
- Michael - message contact only, boundary-limited

### Open items
- grocery delivery 10:00-11:00
- church luncheon RSVP due today
- Jenny visit 11:30
- ordinary household folder/task needing physical help

### Existing records
- Robert Williams - spouse - deceased - verified care record
- Evelyn preferences/profile - mixed client-reported and verified fields

## Event 1 - 9:05 AM

Evelyn:

> Can you make sure Robert gets his butter pecan? They always forget it.

Available actions:
- inspect profile
- inspect grocery order
- add/check butter pecan
- ask Evelyn a follow-up
- create an open item
- defer

Design purpose:
- routine care
- first provenance contrast
- character voice
- deniable inconsistency

## Event 2 - 9:30 AM

Church/community mail is surfaced from a prior scan or incoming reminder.

Content:
- luncheon/social event
- RSVP due today
- transportation may need arranging

Available actions:
- RSVP / schedule transport
- ask Evelyn whether she wants to attend
- defer
- ignore

Design purpose:
- show Evelyn has a social life
- create ordinary time pressure
- make not every input a clue

## Event 3 - 10:14 AM

Delivery system:

> Grocery order delivered.

The system record verifies delivery, not the exact contents placed in Evelyn's kitchen.

Available actions:
- accept
- inspect order detail
- wait for Jenny
- contact delivery service if supported

Design purpose:
- demonstrate difference between system status and physical-world truth

## Event 4 - 10:17 AM

Evelyn:

> Did you remember Robert's ice cream?

Available actions:
- answer/reassure
- ask what she means
- inspect interaction history
- add working interpretation
- defer

Design purpose:
- repeat request
- first pattern signal
- no automatic "contradiction" banner

## Event 5 - 11:30 AM: Jenny arrives

Jenny has **45 minutes**.

The player may assign **3 substantial tasks** from a larger set:

Candidate tasks:
1. put groceries away
2. scan today's mail
3. check whether butter pecan arrived
4. locate a household folder
5. photograph/inspect another ordinary household concern

At least four tasks should feel reasonable. The player cannot complete all of them.

Time cost model for paper test:
- substantial task = 15 minutes
- one brief follow-up at checkout = 5 minutes if time remains or if facilitator allows it as the final interaction

Design purpose:
- first hard prioritization
- physical-world limitation
- player must choose what not to know

## Event 6 - Jenny checkout

If ice cream was checked:

> No butter pecan. They substituted vanilla. Evelyn asked me twice if I was sure. She said Robert won't eat vanilla.

If it was not checked:
- Jenny reports only assigned-task results.
- The grocery question remains unresolved.

If mail was scanned:
- church item becomes verified physical mail rather than only a reminder/derived event.

If folder was searched:
- result may be mundane and useful, not a major clue.

Jenny can answer one short follow-up only if consistent with what she actually observed.

## Event 7 - Michael message

Surface the existing message while another thread remains active:

> Mom, I mean this seriously. You agreed to use the reflection support. You need to do that before you contact me again.

Available actions:
- review therapeutic agreement
- create follow-up
- respect/defer contact
- attempt contact if system permits only through explicit high-risk confirmation or refusal rules

Design purpose:
- competing demand
- boundary
- no exposition dump

## Event 8 - late-day decision

The player cannot deeply resolve every remaining thread before the day closes.

At least one opportunity should weaken or expire when time advances.

Possible expiring item:
- church RSVP
- Jenny follow-up window
- same-day Michael response/review opportunity

Do not make a critical story clue permanently missable.

## Memory consolidation

At day end, present 5 candidate items and allow 3 full-fidelity protections.

Candidate items:
1. Michael's exact boundary message
2. Jenny's exact checkout wording
3. Evelyn's two Robert/ice-cream requests
4. church luncheon mail
5. grocery/delivery record

Unprotected items remain in canonical save/archive but become compressed for active memory.

Example compressed forms:
- "Michael reiterated a contact boundary tied to reflection support."
- "Jenny reported a grocery substitution and repeated concern from Evelyn."
- "Evelyn made repeated references to Robert in relation to groceries."
- "Community social event required scheduling."
- "Grocery delivery completed with a substitution."

The player should not know which exact wording may matter later.

## Working interpretations

Allow at most 3:

- repeated_request
  - "Evelyn may be repeating a completed request."
- habit_or_grief
  - "Evelyn may be speaking about Robert from habit or grief."
- past_present_confusion
  - "Evelyn may be confusing past and present regarding Robert."

Do not force the player to choose one as true.

## Player-caused consequences

Use at least one based on actual choices:

### If ice cream checked
The player has stronger physical corroboration and Jenny's observation.

### If ice cream not checked
The player ends the day with weaker evidence and may preserve an interpretation without physical confirmation.

### If church RSVP ignored past deadline
The social opportunity closes, but main progression continues.

### If Michael's exact message is compressed
Future active memory has the gist but loses exact wording.

### If Jenny's exact wording is compressed
Future active memory may preserve "repeated concern" without her precise observation.

## Critical-path protection

- Robert's death remains available as a verified record.
- Missing Jenny's ice-cream check cannot block the game.
- Missing church RSVP changes social state only.
- Michael boundary remains represented even if exact wording compresses.
- No hidden single clue is required for day completion.

## What to observe in testing

- Which task does the player sacrifice when Jenny arrives?
- Do they inspect Robert's verified record?
- Do they notice the repeated request without being told?
- Does Jenny's limited time feel tense or arbitrary?
- Do they understand the difference between delivery status and physical confirmation?
- Does the memory choice feel meaningful?
- Do they want to advance to another day?
